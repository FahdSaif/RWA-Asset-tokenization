#  Web3 Founders Market Validation Interview

As part of our ongoing RWA Tokenization project and broader Web3 research, we are conducting a **founder-focused interview** to understand how builders across the ecosystem validate their ideas, test product-market fit, and navigate early traction challenges.

---

##  Purpose

This interview will help us:

- Understand how Web3 founders gather market feedback.
- Explore real-world techniques used to validate tokenized or on-chain applications.
- Identify gaps in tooling, research support, and early customer insights.
- Inform the development of shared infrastructure that helps founders go from idea to traction faster.

---

##  Interview Themes

The questions focus on:

- How you currently validate your product or protocol idea.
- Which metrics or signals you consider as early proof of demand.
- Pain points in reaching first users or customers.
- Use (or lack) of pre-sales, interviews, or market experiments.
- Your openness to participating in shared founder feedback loops or tooling.

---

##  Who Should Participate?

This interview is ideal for:

- Web3 startup founders and co-founders
- DAO operators
- Builders working on DeFi, NFTs, RWA, AI, or crypto tooling
- Anyone in pre-launch or early-launch stage
