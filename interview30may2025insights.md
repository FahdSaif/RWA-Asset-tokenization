# 📊 Insights from Industry Interviews — Mineral & Gemstone Liquidity

This page summarizes early findings from our industry interviews with stakeholders involved in mineral, gemstone, and other physical asset markets. These interviews guide the design of our blockchain-based Real World Asset (RWA) tokenization platform.

---

## 💡 Key Objectives of the Interviews

- Understand how asset owners currently access liquidity.
- Identify challenges in global fundraising or investment access.
- Measure interest in tokenization, digitization, and legal simplification.
- Discover patterns in pain points related to trust, verification, or transparency.

---

## 🧭 Participant Overview

- Respondents include asset owners, brokers, and legal professionals across:
  - Minerals and ores (e.g. lithium, gold, rare earths)
  - Precious stones (e.g. diamonds)
  - Export/import traders
  - Fundraisers in emerging markets

---

## 📉 Key Challenges Shared

| Challenge                                     | % of Respondents Reporting It |
|----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Difficulty accessing global investors         | High                          |
| Reliance on outdated or manual legal processes| High                          |
| High cost/time to secure funding              | Moderate                      |
| Investor trust in asset value/authenticity    | Very High                     |
| Lack of standardized, compliant infrastructure| High                          |

> “Even with valuable stockpiles, proving asset legitimacy to outside investors is hard.”

> “We use legal contracts and brokers. It's slow, expensive, and difficult to scale.”

> “Cross-border deals are blocked by paperwork and trust gaps.”

---

## 📈 What Asset Owners Want

- A trusted, digital way to prove asset value
- Access to **tokenized fundraising** models
- Legal simplification for onboarding new investors
- Visibility to **global investors and crypto capital**
- Optional KYC-AML screening baked into the system

---

## 🔍 Validation of Blockchain Use

> ✅ Over 80% of respondents said they would explore blockchain-based platforms if:
> - Legal compliance is built-in
> - Their asset value can be certified digitally
> - It helps them reach international investors

---

## 📂 What’s Next?

- We will use these insights to define the MVP scope for:
  - Tokenized fundraising
  - Proof-of-ownership and verification frameworks
  - KYC-compatible investor onboarding
  - Legal wrappers and compliance tools
