# 📊 Insights from Web3 Founder Interviews

Below is a summary of responses from our ongoing Web3 founder interviews related to market validation and early traction in crypto projects.

---

##  Common Validation Methods

- 65% use direct customer interviews
- 40% rely on landing page + waitlist metrics
- 25% test token utility via simulations or gated Discords

---

##  Key Pain Points

- Difficulty in identifying qualified early users
- Low response rate for traditional Web2-style surveys
- Hard to measure actual interest without a working token or MVP

---

##  Signals Considered Valid by Founders

| Signal                         | % of Founders |
|-------------------------------|---------------|
| Wallet signups or on-chain tx | 55%           |
| Interview confirmations       | 45%           |
| Community activity (Discord)  | 30%           |
| Pre-sales                     | 20%           |

---

##  Selected Anonymous Quotes

> “We struggled to prove demand until we airdropped something useful — then got clarity.”

> “No one cared about our idea until we wrapped it in an NFT utility — then feedback changed overnight.”

> “Our tokenomics experiment helped us validate user behavior in DeFi before launch.”
