# 🌍 Real World Asset (RWA) Tokenization Platform

Welcome to the RWA Tokenization project!

This repository is the foundation of our journey toward building a next-generation platform for **tokenizing real-world assets (RWAs)** such as minerals, food products, diamonds, or industrial goods using blockchain technology.

---

## 🧭 Project Vision

We aim to bring **trust, traceability, and liquidity** to physical assets by tokenizing them on-chain.

Depending on our final architectural direction, we will either:

- **Build our own blockchain** tailored specifically for RWA compliance, traceability, and regulation,  
**OR**
- **Use existing blockchain infrastructure** (e.g. Ethereum, Polymesh, VeChain, Hyperledger Fabric) to power our application.

We may also start with a **multi-chain approach** for greater flexibility and interoperability.

---

## 🚧 Project Scope

We're currently evaluating two core directions:

### 1. Supply Chain to Tokenization
Build an end-to-end vertical stack — from source to certified product — capturing every step of the supply chain (e.g. mining, processing, transport, verification), and tokenizing the verified asset.

### 2. Tokenization at Endpoint
Focus only on tokenizing the **final product** once it’s verified or certified, without tracking the upstream supply chain.

---

## 🔗 Blockchain Options Under Consideration

- **Ethereum** – For ERC-20, ERC-721, ERC-1155, and ERC-1400 tokens
- **Polymesh** – Purpose-built for security tokens and compliance
- **VeChain** – Ideal for supply chain and provenance
- **Hyperledger Fabric** – Permissioned blockchain for enterprise and consortium use cases
- **Custom Blockchain** – Purpose-built chain for RWAs with native compliance modules
- **Multi-Chain Setup** – Bridging multiple blockchains for cross-chain RWA tokenization

---

## 🛠 Planned Features

- Asset minting and ownership transfer via smart contracts
- KYC/AML integration and role-based access control
- Fractional ownership and regulated security token support
- Off-chain/on-chain metadata mapping and audit trails
- Legal wrapper considerations for tokenized assets
- Asset redemption and lifecycle management
