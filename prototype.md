##  Future Enhancements

We plan to integrate:

- Blockchain deployment via Ethereum/Polygon smart contracts
- Legal wrapper generation (e.g. for ERC-1400 compliance)
- NFT and metadata linking
- Wallet-based issuance and transfer

This prototype demonstrates the core flow of **tokenizing real-world assets (RWAs)** into fractional tokens. It walks users through selecting an asset, breaking it into digital shares, and preparing it for issuance to stakeholders or investors.
