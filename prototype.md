This screen guides users through the KYC (Know Your Customer) identity verification step. Users are prompted to upload high-quality images of both the front and back of a government-issued ID (e.g., passport or driver’s license). The left-hand panel shows the full verification progress, including Personal Details, Address Verification, and Final Review. This step is essential before accessing any investment features on the platform.
