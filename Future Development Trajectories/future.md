# Future Development Trajectories

We have identified a number of future research directions and innovative development trajectories. They include standardisation, modularised compliance architecture, and real-time auditing mechanisms. 

## Standardisation Initiatives
Global standards organisations including the **International Organization for Standardization (ISO)** and the **Enterprise Ethereum Alliance (EEA)** are actively developing frameworks for digital asset metadata, auditability, and compliance logic.  
The adoption of token standards such as **ERC-1400**, which supports partitioned ownership and regulatory modules, alongside regulatory frameworks like the **FATF Travel Rule**, is expected to significantly improve cross-border operability and regulatory clarity for tokenised assets.

## Composable Compliance Architecture
New smart contract architectures are enforcing modular compliance.  
Emerging composable compliance architectures integrate KYC, AML, and audit controls directly into smart contracts, enabling protocols to enforce dynamic risk-based rules based on user profiles and transaction contexts whilst maintaining decentralisation principles.

## Institutional Infrastructure Development
Maturing custody solutions, reporting tools, and legacy finance integration facilitate institutional adoption.  
Platforms like **Fireblocks** and **Anchorage** provide secure infrastructure tailored for asset managers and banks, enabling compliant real-world asset onboarding.

## Real-Time Auditing and Verification
Zero-knowledge proofs (ZKPs) and Proof-of-Solvency models support continuous monitoring.  
Advances in zero-knowledge proofs, homomorphic encryption, and real-time data feeds enable continuous tokenised asset auditing. Proof-of-reserve and proof-of-solvency models are becoming standard features, increasing trust in real-world asset protocols.

## Programmable Finance and Derivative Products
RWAs are enabling new composable instruments across markets.  
Tokenised real-world assets enable new programmable derivatives, structured products, and real-time asset streaming capabilities. By integrating real-world triggers including interest rate changes and environmental data, DeFi protocols can launch sophisticated instruments previously limited to institutional trading desks.
